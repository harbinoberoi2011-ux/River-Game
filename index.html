What is a River?

A river is a flowing body of water that begins at a source and ends at a mouth. Rivers provide water, transportation, fertile soil, food, and trade routes.

The Nile River

The Nile helped support ancient civilizations by providing water, fertile floodplains, transportation, and trade opportunities. Cities and farms developed along its banks for thousands of years.
